Surfaces

The baseline cross-fade cannot reach an unbound teaser, or an occupied-slot teaser once "Allow a pouring beer to be cross-faded out" is off. Those are the overflow; a surface is where they can live.

{% call unit('show_upcoming_deck_page') %}

A dedicated page joining the carousel, carrying whatever this scope resolves to. It rotates like any other page - same timing, its own dot - and is not shown at all when there is nothing to carry.

{% endcall %} {% call unit('show_upcoming_panel') %}

A panel spanning the full width across the bottom half of the board, carrying whatever this scope resolves to. Reads as an interruption (lighter ground, dashed border, drop shadow) rather than as board furniture. Not shown at all when there is nothing to carry.

{% endcall %} {% call unit('upcoming_panel_multiple') %}

A panel turn takes a tick the cross-fade would otherwise use, so a low multiple means the pouring-tap teasers spend more of their turns waiting behind the panel. With the on-deck page also enabled, the panel skips any turn that would land on top of it - the page already shows everything the panel would.

{% endcall %} {% call unit('upcoming_surface_scope') %}

"Overflow only" never shows a beer twice at once. "All upcoming" includes a teaser already pinned permanently to a Vacant Slot, so a dedicated surface is genuinely complete - the same beer may then appear in two places, at different moments, which is expected.

{% endcall %}

Scales the beer photo and the card text on the display, for screens the defaults do not suit. Photo scale only shrinks - the photo already fills the space the card text leaves it, so there is nothing above Default to give it. A larger text preset takes room from the photo, by design.
